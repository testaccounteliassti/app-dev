# 🎬 My Favorite Series: *Breaking Bad*

> "A chemistry teacher's double life changes everything."

## 📖 About the Show
**Breaking Bad** is an American crime drama that aired from **2008 to 2013**.
It follows *Walter White*, a high school chemistry teacher who turns to crime
after a life-changing diagnosis.

---

## 👥 Main Characters
1. **Walter White** – chemistry teacher
2. **Jesse Pinkman** – Walter's former student and partner
3. **Skyler White** – Walter's wife
4. **Hank Schrader** – DEA agent and Walter's brother-in-law

## ⭐ Why I Like It
- Strong character development
- Great acting, especially by ***Bryan Cranston*** and ***Aaron Paul***
- Every season builds more tension
  - Season 5 is my favorite
  - The ending is satisfying
